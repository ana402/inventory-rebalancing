📦 Inventory Rebalancing Tool

Pipeline Cleaning & Transfer Analysis
Upload one or multiple Pipeline Excel files from SAP. The tool combines the files, cleans the information, calculates inventory coverage and identifies transfer opportunities across plants.

How it works

1. Upload Pipeline files
Upload one or multiple SAP Pipeline files from the plants you want to compare. All files are combined into one analysis.
2. Data cleaning
The tool keeps only the required fields such as Material, Plant, MRP Controller, Unrestricted Stock, Current Annual Rate, Net Price, Vendor Price Unit and Currency.
3. Inventory coverage
Inventory Years is calculated as:
Unrestricted Stock ÷ Current Annual Rate
4. Source plant rule
A plant is considered a potential source only when it has more than 5 years of inventory.
5. Minimum stock kept at source
The source plant must keep at least 2 years of inventory after the transfer.
6. Target plant rule
A receiving plant must have less than 1 year of inventory and a Current Annual Rate of at least 100 units.
7. Suggested transfer quantity
The suggested quantity is the lower value between:

• Quantity required to bring the target plant to 1 year of inventory.
• Quantity available at the source while keeping 2 years of inventory.
8. Minimum transfer impact
The opportunity is reported only when the suggested quantity covers at least 30 days of demand at the receiving plant.
9. Priority
Critical: 180 days or more
High: 90–179 days
Medium: 30–89 days
10. Price normalization
Unit Price is calculated as:
Net Price ÷ Vendor Price Unit
11. Potential Saving
Potential Saving is calculated only when both plants have valid price information and use the same currency.

(Target Unit Price − Source Unit Price) × Suggested Transfer Qty

Only positive savings are reported.
12. Final output
Download the generated Excel file and use the Possible Transfers sheet to update the Google Sheet connected to Looker Studio.
